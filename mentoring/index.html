SDE 求职辅导

我是一名有约 5 年经验的软件工程师，做过后端研发、GIS 地理空间系统、金融科技风控系统，也做过技术产品经理。我在英国读研，在海外参加过大量技术面试，熟悉企业真实的研发场景，也知道面试官想听到什么。

我的辅导重点不是背题，而是帮你把真实做过的事讲清楚、讲出深度。

服务内容

  • 简历与项目经历优化
    找出简历里最有价值的项目，按”背景、你的角色、技术方案、结果”重写，让每一条都经得起追问。

  • 项目深挖
    模拟面试官逐层追问你的项目：为什么这么设计？瓶颈在哪？如果数据量翻十倍怎么办？帮你提前准备好答案。

  • 系统设计面试
    从需求澄清、容量估算、接口设计、数据模型到扩展性和取舍，带你建立一套能套用到任何题目的答题框架。

  • 模拟技术面试（Mock Interview）
    按目标公司的面试流程完整模拟，面试结束后给你逐项的书面反馈和改进建议。

  • 技术知识体系梳理
    针对后端、数据库、分布式、Python / Java 等方向，帮你找出知识盲区，制定复习计划。

  • 职业发展规划
    后端、GIS、金融科技、AI 应用、技术产品，这些方向怎么选、怎么转，结合你的背景一起分析。

适合谁

  • 准备找 SDE / 后端开发岗位的应届生和在读硕士
  • 想从其他岗位转向软件开发的工程师
  • 有工作经验、准备跳槽，但不知道如何讲好项目的开发者
  • 对 GIS、金融科技、AI 应用方向感兴趣，想了解行业真实情况的同学

辅导流程

  1. 发邮件预约：附上简历和目标岗位，简单说明你目前最想解决的问题。
  2. 首次沟通：了解你的背景和目标，确定辅导重点和节奏。
  3. 针对性辅导：简历修改、项目深挖、系统设计或模拟面试，按需安排。
  4. 复盘反馈：每次辅导后给出书面反馈，标出进步和下一步要练的地方。

预约方式

发邮件到 kentanvictor@foxmail.com，标题写”SDE 辅导 + 你的名字”。我会尽快回复。也可以在 LinkedIn 上联系我。

SDE Career Mentoring

I am a software engineer with about five years of experience across backend development, GIS and geospatial systems, FinTech risk systems and technical product management. I did my master’s in the UK and have been through many technical interviews abroad, so I know what real engineering work looks like and what interviewers want to hear.

My mentoring is not about memorising answers. It is about helping you explain what you have actually built, clearly and in depth.

What I offer

  • Resume and project review
    Find the most valuable projects on your resume and rewrite them as context, your role, technical approach and outcome, so every line holds up under follow-up questions.

  • Project deep dives
    I play the interviewer and keep digging into your project: why did you design it this way? Where is the bottleneck? What if the data grew tenfold? You prepare the answers before the real interview.

  • System design interviews
    From clarifying requirements, capacity estimates, API design and data models to scalability and trade-offs, you build a framework you can apply to any question.

  • Mock technical interviews
    A full mock interview following your target company’s process, followed by detailed written feedback and concrete suggestions.

  • Knowledge mapping
    For backend, databases, distributed systems, Python, Java and more, we find your gaps and plan your revision.

  • Career direction
    Backend, GIS, FinTech, AI applications or technical product management: we look at your background together and work out which path fits and how to get there.

Who it is for

  • Graduates and master’s students preparing for SDE or backend roles
  • Engineers moving into software development from other roles
  • Experienced developers changing jobs who struggle to present their projects
  • Anyone curious about GIS, FinTech or AI applications who wants an inside view of the industry

How it works

  1. Email to book: attach your resume and target role, and tell me briefly what you most want to work on.
  2. First conversation: we go through your background and goals and agree on the focus and pace.
  3. Focused sessions: resume work, project deep dives, system design or mock interviews, as needed.
  4. Review and feedback: after each session you get written feedback on what improved and what to practise next.

Get in touch

Email kentanvictor@foxmail.com with the subject “SDE Mentoring + your name”. I will get back to you as soon as I can. You can also reach me on LinkedIn.

Want to know more about me first? Have a look at About Me and the technical posts on this blog.
