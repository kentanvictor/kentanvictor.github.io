## Hi, I'm Zoe

**Software Engineer · Backend / System Design / GIS / FinTech / AI Applications**

I have about five years of experience in software development and enterprise projects. I have built geospatial information systems and financial risk systems, and I have led an industry digital transformation project as a product manager.
